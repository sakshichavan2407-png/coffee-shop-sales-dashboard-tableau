# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyses sales for a coffee shop chain across five US cities: Austin, Chicago, Los Angeles, New York and Seattle. It helps answer where revenue and profit come from, which products and branches perform best, and when customers buy the most.

## Features
- **5 KPI cards:** Total Revenue, Total Profit, Units Sold, Average Order Value and Profit Margin %
- **Trend analysis:** revenue and profit over time
- **City share:** donut chart of revenue by city
- **Product mix:** revenue by product category
- **Weekday patterns:** revenue by day of the week
- **Profit heatmap:** city × product category
- **Branch performance:** treemap and Pareto chart showing how much revenue the top branches drive
- **Global filters:** City, Product Category, Weekday and Year update every chart at once

## Tools
Tableau Desktop, Excel (data source)

## How to use
1. Open `tableau/Coffee_Shop_Dashboard.twbx` in Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public.
2. Use the filters to explore the data. The data extract is packaged inside the file, so nothing else is needed.
